VortexCause — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

VortexCause is a co-op tower-defense game. This policy explains what data the game processes when you sign in and play online. We keep this to the minimum needed to run multiplayer.

Who we are

VortexCause is developed by an independent developer. For any privacy questions, contact: martin@eero.dk.

What we process

  • Epic account info — your Epic Account ID and display name, obtained via Epic Online Services (EOS) when you sign in. Used to identify you in a game session.
  • Friends & presence — with your consent, your Epic friends list and online presence, used only so you can invite friends and join their games.
  • Session data — temporary connection/session information needed to host and join multiplayer matches (peer-to-peer via EOS).

How we use it

Only to provide multiplayer functionality: signing in, hosting/joining games, and inviting friends. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it except as needed to operate the game through Epic Online Services.

Third parties

The game uses Epic Online Services (Epic Games) for login, sessions, friends, and networking. Your use of EOS is also governed by Epic's own privacy policy and terms.

Data retention

Session and presence data is transient and not retained beyond your play session. We do not maintain our own separate database of player accounts.

Your choices

Friends and presence access is optional and consent-based. You can decline it (invites simply won't be available), and you can stop using the online features at any time.

Children

The game is not directed at children under the age required by Epic's terms in your region.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date above will reflect any changes.
